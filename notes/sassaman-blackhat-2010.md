- Reading:
  - No direct alibi: Satoshi was silent exactly during the talk and active before and after.
  - But on those two days the Satoshi mail and build machines were on **London time** while Sassaman was in Las Vegas, and his home was Belgium (CEST, +0200). For Sassaman to be the operator, he'd need a laptop set to London time, which is neither his home zone nor his location. That's possible (deliberate or a remote UK machine), but it's an extra assumption.
  - The packaging folder used for 0.3.6 was created 26 Jun 2010 and reused through 0.3.19 (Dec 2010), consistent with one fixed machine.
  - INFERENCE: weak-to-moderate evidence against Sassaman as the day-to-day operator in July 2010.
