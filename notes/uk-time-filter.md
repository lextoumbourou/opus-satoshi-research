# How strong is the "UK time" filter? (VERIFIED counts; INFERENCE on meaning)

Script: `scripts/list_author_timezones.py`. Data: metzdowd Cryptography list 2008-01 → 2010-10 and randombit Cryptography list 2010-11 → 2011-06, `Date:` headers.

- 140 posters with ≥5 dated posts. Clean **UK/IE/PT rule (+0000 winter / +0100 summer): 10 (7%)**. CET/CEST 14 (10%), US Eastern 13 (9%), US Pacific 10 (7%). 44% "mixed" (travel, several machines, webmail), 18% seen in one season only.
- So the machine-time evidence narrows Satoshi's operator to a group of roughly one-in-ten of the relevant community (more if "mixed" people are included). **It is a filter, not an identification.**
