# Sources for material not included in this repository

This repository contains our own notes, scripts, logs and derived data, plus public community archives (mailing lists, forums, emails published by their recipients), Satoshi's writing, the blockchain and open-source software.

Published works we consulted are **not** included: news and magazine articles, blog essays, academic papers, court filings, images and screenshots from articles, video transcripts, tweets and Wikipedia copies. They were removed from the repository and its history. Each is listed in [`sources.csv`](sources.csv) with its original path in our notes, its size, its SHA-256 (so you can check a copy you download against the one we used) and where to get it.

Web pages change, so a fresh download of a live page may not match the recorded hash. Where a Wayback Machine capture is listed, that capture is the version we used. Our notes quote these sources only briefly, for commentary.
